How you're doing

The Koch method starts at full character speed with just two characters. Copy a session at 90% or better and the next character joins. Hearing whole characters at speed, rather than counting dots and dashes, is what gets you past the plateau at 10 wpm. Farnsworth spacing adds time between characters while you learn; close it up as you improve.

?

Hear a character, press its key (or tap it) as fast as you can. Slow and missed characters come back more often.

Reaction times

Play Morse near the microphone, from a radio, a recording or another app. It finds the tone between 300 and 1,200 Hz and follows the speed. It copies clean machine-sent code best; hand-sent code with uneven spacing, noise, or two signals at once will trip it up, as they do any decoder. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Tap any character to hear it. In Koch order: the order you'll learn them.
